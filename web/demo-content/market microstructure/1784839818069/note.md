Imbalance:
- related to the probability of the price moving up
- When imbalance is close to 1, traders are more likely to want to buy
  - Larger buying pressure, thus market makers will likely shift their quotes higher to avoid being picked off by informed buyers
- When imbalance is close to 0, traders are more likely to want to sell
  - Large selling pressure, thus market makers will likely shift their quotes lower to avoid being picked off by informed sellers
